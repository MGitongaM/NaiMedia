# Privacy Policy


At **NaiMedia Kenya** ("we," "our," "us"), accessible from [https://naimediakenya.com/](https://naimediakenya.com), protecting the privacy and personal data of our clients, event planners, corporate partners, and site visitors is a top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, request quotes, lease audio-visual (AV) equipment, or engage our event production services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, improve, and execute our AV rental and event production services. The types of information we collect include:

### A. Personal Identification Data
* **Contact Information:** Name, email address, phone number, physical address, and company name when you request a quote, submit an inquiry, or book equipment.
* **Identity Documents:** National ID or Passport numbers, and company registration details (required for equipment rental agreements and lease verification).

### B. Event & Technical Specifications
* Venue locations, event dates, technical specs, site layouts, and logistics details required for equipment delivery, setup, and structural engineering.

### C. Financial & Billing Information
* Payment details, MPESA payment references, bank transfer records, tax PINs, and billing addresses necessary to process invoices and Security Deposits.

### D. Automated Technical Data
* IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and website interactions collected automatically via cookies when navigating our digital platforms.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

* **Service Delivery:** Executing AV equipment rentals, logistics planning, site surveys, and on-site event engineering.
* **Contractual Fulfillments:** Processing rental agreements, verifying equipment leasing eligibility, and issuing invoices/receipts.
* **Customer Communication:** Responding to quotes, sending project updates, and providing technical support.
* **Security & Asset Protection:** Preventing equipment loss, fraud, and unauthorized equipment re-leasing.
* **Marketing & Updates:** Sending promotional materials or service updates (only where explicit consent has been provided).

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We may disclose your information under the following circumstances:

* **Service Providers & Subcontractors:** Third-party logistics operators, specialized equipment technicians, or venue security teams acting under strict confidentiality obligations.
* **Legal & Regulatory Compliance:** When required by Kenyan law, court orders, or governmental agencies (e.g., Kenya Revenue Authority, law enforcement).
* **Asset Protection:** To legal counsel or insurance entities in the event of lost, stolen, or damaged leased equipment.

---

## 4. Equipment Security Deposits & Data Retention

* Personal and corporate identification collected for equipment leasing is retained for the duration of the rental period and until equipment audit/inspection is complete.
* General transactional records are stored in compliance with Kenyan tax laws and accounting standards (typically up to 7 years).

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to maintain the safety of your personal data. This includes secure database storage, SSL encryption on web forms, restricted data access control, and physical security measures at our warehouse facilities.

---

## 6. Your Data Protection Rights

Under the **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019**, you hold the following rights:

* **Right to Access:** Request copies of the personal data we hold about you.
* **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
* **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your personal data, subject to active contractual or legal retention requirements.
* **Right to Object/Restrict:** Object to direct marketing or request processing restrictions.

To exercise these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at **privacy@naimedia.co.ke**.
